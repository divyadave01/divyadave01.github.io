E-Book Store

HTML/CSS/JSE-CommerceSQLCRUD

The Problem

Readers need a seamless, centralized platform to browse, search, and purchase digital books without the clutter and overhead of physical retail stores.

The Solution

Developed a full-stack e-commerce web application. The platform features a dynamic product catalog that pulls from a database, secure user authentication, and a functional shopping cart that calculates totals in real-time. The database is optimized for fast search queries to ensure a smooth user experience.

The frontend is built to be fully responsive, ensuring that users can browse and purchase books effortlessly from their mobile devices or desktops. The backend handles secure data transactions and inventory management.

What I Learned

Managing state across multiple pages (specifically the shopping cart logic) was a key learning outcome. I also gained valuable experience in designing a normalized relational database schema to handle users, products, and orders efficiently.
