IoT-Based Smart Classroom

Cisco Packet TracerIoTNetworkingSmart Systems

The Problem

Classrooms depend heavily on manual control of lights, fans, and projectors, leading to significant energy waste and inefficiency. There is a lack of real-time monitoring for environmental conditions like temperature and lighting.

The Solution

Designed a simulated smart classroom network where IoT devices (sensors and actuators) are connected to a central gateway. The system allows for remote monitoring and automated control of environmental factors.

For example, lights automatically turn on when motion sensors detect occupancy, and fans adjust speed based on temperature readings. The centralized dashboard provides real-time data on energy consumption and device status.

What I Learned

I gained a deep understanding of IoT network architecture, including edge devices, gateways, and cloud integration. I also learned how to simulate wireless device connectivity in a local network and how to secure IoT endpoints to prevent unauthorized access.
